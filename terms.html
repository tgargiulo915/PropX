Terms and Privacy

Plain-language summary for subscribers. Last updated October 29, 2026.

What PropX is

PropX is a tool that estimates a property's value, rent, taxes and deal numbers from public and licensed data, so investors can decide what to look into further. It is not an appraisal, inspection, title search, or legal, tax, financial or investment advice.

Estimates, not guarantees

  • Values, rents, taxes, comparable sales, permits and flags are automated estimates and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.
  • Always verify figures yourself (appraisal, inspection, county records, a licensed professional) before you make an offer or commit money.
  • Any deal score or verdict is a rough guide based on the numbers you enter. You are responsible for your own decisions and results.

Access keys and use

  • Each subscriber gets a personal access key and a monthly search allowance. Keep your key private and do not share it. We can turn a key off if it is shared, abused or used to collect data in bulk.
  • Searches are limited per month and per minute. Unused searches do not roll over.
  • Do not scrape, resell or republish PropX data, or try to get around the limits.

Liability

PropX is provided "as is", without warranties. To the extent the law allows, PropX and its owner are not liable for losses arising from decisions made using the reports.

Privacy: what we collect

  • Addresses you search are sent to our data providers and public data sources to build the report.
  • Your access key and recent searches are saved in your own browser on your device so the site can remember them. Clear your browser data to remove them.
  • Usage counts (how many searches each key has run this month) are stored on our servers to enforce your plan.
  • Your email, if you request access, is used only to contact you about PropX.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask for payment details on this page.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms as the product changes. If you have a question or want your key turned off or your data removed, use the contact you received your key from.
