        Questions.

        What counts as a search?

        One address lookup. It pulls the value, comps, rent, taxes and flags together. Changing your numbers afterwards is free and unlimited.

        Where does the data come from?

        Property data comes from RentCast, plus public sources such as local permit records where they are available. Everything is an automated estimate.

        How accurate is it?

        It is a starting point, not an appraisal. Values, rents and comps can be wrong or out of date, so verify them before you make an offer. See the terms.

        Can I try it first?

        Yes. Use "Load an example property" in the report above. It shows a full example with made-up data and uses no searches.

        Does it work for wholesaling and rentals?

        Yes. The report has Flip, Rental, Wholesale and BRRRR views that use the same numbers.

        When do searches reset?

        On the first of each month. Unused searches do not roll over.

        How do I cancel?

        Plans are monthly and you can cancel any time. Access ends when the period you paid for ends.
